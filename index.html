Who We Are

A Passion for Winter Light

We are a collective of photographers obsessed with winter's silent palette — the blue-grey of dawn frost, the gold that lingers in bare branches, the mirror-still surfaces of iced-over lakes.

Every photograph in this gallery is shot on location in the world's coldest seasons, with no studio trickery — only patience, timing, and a deep love for the cold.
